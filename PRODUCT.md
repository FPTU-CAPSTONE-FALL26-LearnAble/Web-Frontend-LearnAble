# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

LearnAble serves university students, including blind and visually impaired students learning in inclusive mainstream environments, together with lecturers, examiners, and university administrators.

## Product Purpose

LearnAble provides an accessible university learning and examination environment. It brings learning materials, question authoring, exam preparation, scheduling, accessible practice, examination, monitoring, and results into one system.

## Positioning

LearnAble connects accessible student experiences with the staff workflows that prepare, operate, and govern university assessment, so accessibility is part of the academic workflow rather than a separate accommodation added afterward.

## Operating Context

Students use LearnAble for accessible learning, practice, exams, progress, and results. Lecturers prepare questions, materials, schedules, and attendance. Examiners prepare papers and schedules and oversee exam operations. Administrators maintain the academic catalog and user records.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and canonical shadcn/Radix primitives under `src/shared/ui/primitives`.
- The public landing page introduces the inclusion problem, LearnAble's solution, and ways to access the system.
- Authentication and role workbenches remain separate application surfaces.
- Claims about education and disability must be supported by credible public sources; testimonials, customers, and outcomes must not be invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must preserve keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive alternative text, reduced-motion behavior, and strong color contrast. Blind and visually impaired students are a primary user group, not an edge case.
